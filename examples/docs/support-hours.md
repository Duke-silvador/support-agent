# Support hours and response times

Standard support runs **Monday to Friday, 08:00–18:00 CET**, excluding public holidays
in Germany.

## Response-time targets

| Plan | First response | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 1 business day | 5 business days |
| Business | 4 business hours | 2 business days |
| Enterprise | 1 hour, 24/7 | 8 hours |

## Escalation

If a ticket is not resolved within its target, it is escalated automatically to the
on-call engineer. Customers on the Enterprise plan also get a dedicated Slack channel.
