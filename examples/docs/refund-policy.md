# Refund policy

Acme Cloud offers a **30-day money-back guarantee** on new subscriptions. You can
request a refund within 30 days of your first charge, and it is returned to the
original payment method within 5–7 business days.

## Annual plans

Annual plans are refundable pro-rata for unused full months, minus any discount
already applied. The current month is not refunded.

## How to request a refund

Open the Billing page, choose the invoice, and click "Request refund". Refunds over
$2,000 require approval from a finance manager and can take up to 3 extra business
days.

## Exclusions

Add-on usage (extra API calls, premium support hours) is non-refundable once consumed.
