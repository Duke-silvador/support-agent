# Refund policy

New subscriptions can be refunded within **30 days** of the first charge. The money
is returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

Annual plans are refunded pro-rata for unused full months, minus any discount already
applied. Add-on usage is non-refundable once consumed.

Refunds above 2,000 USD need approval from a finance manager and take up to three
extra business days.
